# Biomes Unseen

A Forge 1.20.1 mod with 80 Overworld biomes, the **Hollow Deep** dimension (60 biomes), 85 animated
hostile mobs, a three-phase boss, 44 ores and 115 structures.

**Requires:** Forge 47.x, GeckoLib 4, TerraBlender.

## What you get

- 80 Overworld biomes in ten families, blended in with TerraBlender
- The Hollow Deep: twelve themed biome families, from frost and crystal to void and ember
- 85 GeckoLib mobs, each with a spawn egg, on-hit effect and drops
- The Heart of the Forest boss: three phases, ranged attacks, minions, phase-aware boss bar
- 44 ores with smelting and blasting recipes
- 115 structures: towers, ruins, shrines, crypts, camps and more, with loot chests

## Try it

No portal yet:

```
/execute in biomesunseen:hollow_deep run tp @s 0 100 0
```
