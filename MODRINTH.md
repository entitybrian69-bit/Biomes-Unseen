# Biomes Unseen

A Forge 1.20.1 mod with 80 naturally blended Overworld biomes, a 60-biome dimension called the **Hollow Deep**, 86 hostile mobs, a three-phase boss, 44 ores, weapons, and 115 structures.

**Requires:** Forge 47.x, GeckoLib 4 for 1.20.1, and TerraBlender for 1.20.1.

## Features

- 80 Overworld biomes in ten families, injected by TerraBlender
- Hollow Deep: twelve themed biome families
- 86 hostile mobs and the Heart of the Forest boss
- 44 ores with drops, smelting, and blasting recipes
- 45 weapons: 44 ore-material swords and the Hollowite Battleaxe
- 115 structures, including ruins, shrines, towers, camps, and the boss arena
- In-game Field Guide and reusable Hollow Sigil for dimension travel

## Try it

Create a **new world** after installing the mod; old chunks are not regenerated. The Field Guide is craftable with a book, paper, and compass. The Hollow Sigil is craftable from obsidian, ender pearls, glowstone dust, and paper; use it to travel to or from the Hollow Deep.

With cheats enabled, check world generation with:

```text
/locate biome biomesunseen:amber_meadow
/locate structure biomesunseen:windmill_ruin
/execute in biomesunseen:hollow_deep run tp @s 0 100 0
```

Audio is placeholder silence. The Hollow Deep has a sigil, not a portal block.
