# Biomes Unseen

Eighty new Overworld biomes, a lightless cavern dimension called the **Hollow Deep**, and an army of
animated creatures that live in both.

## Features

- **80 Overworld biomes** in ten families: meadows, forests, highlands, wetlands, deserts, cold lands, jungles, coasts, fantasy wilds and volcanic lands.
- **The Hollow Deep** — a dimension with 60 biomes across twelve themes: heartwood, glowcap, crystal, ashen, mire, frost, bone, sunken, rust, void, coral and ember.
- **85 hostile mobs** — every one fully animated with GeckoLib, each with its own colours, stats, on-hit effect and drop, plus a spawn egg.
- **The Heart of the Forest** — a three-phase boss with thorn volleys, summoned minions and a boss bar that changes as the fight escalates.
- **44 ores** — gems, ingots, shards and crystals in the Overworld and the deep, with smelting and blasting recipes.
- **115 structures** — towers, ruins, shrines, huts, wells, bridges, camps, crypts, obelisks and stone circles, many with loot chests and guards.

## Requirements

- Minecraft 1.20.1
- Forge 47.x
- GeckoLib 4
- TerraBlender

## Getting to the Hollow Deep

Portals are not in this version. Use `/execute in biomesunseen:hollow_deep run tp @s 0 100 0`.
