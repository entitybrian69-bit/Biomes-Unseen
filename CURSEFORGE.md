# Biomes Unseen

A Minecraft 1.20.1 Forge mod with 80 naturally blended Overworld biomes, the **Hollow Deep** dimension (60 biomes), 86 hostile mobs, a three-phase boss, 44 ores, weapons, and 115 structures.

## Features

- **80 Overworld biomes** across ten families, naturally blended into new worlds through TerraBlender.
- **The Hollow Deep** — a dimension with 60 biomes across twelve themes.
- **86 hostile mobs** — 80 generated creatures, five hand-written mobs, and the Heart of the Forest boss.
- **The Heart of the Forest** — a three-phase boss with thorn volleys, summoned minions, and a phase-changing boss bar.
- **44 ores** — gems, ingots, shards, and crystals in the Overworld and Hollow Deep.
- **45 weapons** — a sword for each ore material plus the Hollowite Battleaxe.
- **115 structures** — ruins, shrines, towers, camps, and the boss arena.
- An in-game **Field Guide**, reusable **Hollow Sigil**, and craftable **Hollow Portal** block for two-way dimension travel.
- **42 original synthesized sound effects** for creatures, the boss, and portal travel.

## Requirements

- Minecraft 1.20.1
- Forge 47.x
- GeckoLib 4 for 1.20.1
- TerraBlender for 1.20.1

## Getting started

Create a **new world** after installing the mod and dependencies; old chunks are not regenerated. Craft the Field Guide from a book, paper, and compass. For travel, craft a Hollow Sigil from obsidian, ender pearls, glowstone dust, and paper, or craft a Hollow Portal from 4 obsidian, 4 amethyst shards, and an ender eye. Place the portal block and walk into it. Both routes connect the Overworld and Hollow Deep.

With cheats enabled, use `/locate biome biomesunseen:amber_meadow` or `/locate structure biomesunseen:windmill_ruin` to search newly generated terrain. The backup dimension command is `/execute in biomesunseen:hollow_deep run tp @s 0 100 0`.

The seven secondary locales translate the Field Guide and core gameplay items; most biome, mob, and structure names remain English. The sound effects are original procedural synths rather than recordings.
