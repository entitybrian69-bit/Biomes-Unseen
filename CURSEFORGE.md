# Biomes Unseen

A Minecraft 1.20.1 Forge mod with 80 Overworld biomes, the **Hollow Deep** dimension (60 biomes), 86 hostile mobs, a three-phase boss, 44 ores, weapons, and 115 structures.

## Features

- **80 Overworld biomes** across ten families, naturally blended into new worlds through TerraBlender.
- **The Hollow Deep** — a dimension with 60 biomes across twelve themes.
- **86 hostile mobs** — 80 generated creatures, five hand-written mobs, and the Heart of the Forest boss.
- **The Heart of the Forest** — a three-phase boss with thorn volleys, summoned minions, and a phase-changing boss bar.
- **44 ores** — gems, ingots, shards, and crystals in the Overworld and Hollow Deep.
- **45 weapons** — a sword for each ore material plus the Hollowite Battleaxe.
- **115 structures** — towers, ruins, shrines, crypts, camps, and more, with loot chests.
- An in-game **Field Guide** and a reusable **Hollow Sigil** for dimension travel.

## Requirements

- Minecraft 1.20.1
- Forge 47.x
- GeckoLib 4 for 1.20.1
- TerraBlender for 1.20.1

## Getting started

Create a **new world** after installing the mod and dependencies; existing chunks do not regenerate. Craft the Field Guide from a book, paper, and compass. Craft the Hollow Sigil from obsidian, ender pearls, glowstone dust, and paper. Use the sigil in the Overworld to enter Hollow Deep and again there to return.

With cheats enabled, use `/locate biome biomesunseen:amber_meadow` or `/locate structure biomesunseen:windmill_ruin` to search newly generated terrain. The backup dimension command is `/execute in biomesunseen:hollow_deep run tp @s 0 100 0`.

Sounds are silent placeholders, and the dimension uses a travel item rather than a portal block.
